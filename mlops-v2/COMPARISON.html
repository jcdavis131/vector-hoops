Shared honesty facts (both demos agree — coordinator-verified)

  • Curry 2016-17 → Ginobili 2009-10 is a 14-d stat-space Twin Reveal pair (cosine 0.9623, rank 1). In the 64-d champion embedding space it is cosine 0.4003, rank 390th (person-excluded); the 64-d top twin is James Harden 2017-18 at 0.86. Neither demo presents the pair as a 64-d top twin.
  • The saved mtnn.onnx is 119→48, not the served 64-d v5; mtnn_attr_pop.json and mtnn_jacobian.json are stale 48-d pre-v5 exports marked not_from_served_net. Neither demo uses them as v5 attributions.
  • Steering directions (A) and counterfactual directions (B) are correlations derived from real data (skill-grade embedding means, archetype centroids, stat deciles) — not model internals, not interventions inside the model.
