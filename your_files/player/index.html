Construct validity — plain English

Construct: staying on floor late = trusted by winning coach in last 6 min of close playoff games (margin ≤8).

Operationalized via:
• closing_risk = low/mid/high estimated from switchability, FT%, TO%, foul rate.
• matchup_factor 1.28 closer / 1.12 starter / 1.0 neutral / 0.81 dependent / 0.62 exploitable / multiplied onto playoff_impact_proxy.
• Min filter: closing minutes need 3-seed playoff possession data; rookies get 0 until proven.
