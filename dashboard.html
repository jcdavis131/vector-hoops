02 Clean — Era-honest σ ±4 + family masking + Procrustes root frame

pipeline/era_procrustes_align.py • 30 seasons aligned

Raw stats drift: pace 91 in 1999 vs 100 in 2025, 3PT rate 18% vs 41%. We z-score each stat within its season → era-honest ±4 clip. Then family masking cat([x·m,m]) preserves "never measured" vs zero. Procrustes: chain yearly rotations RᵀR=I back to 1996 root frame — lets 1999 directly compare 2025 without drift. No SOTA tricks, just honest geometry.

  • ✅ Per-season σ — every stat vs its league ±4σ cap
  • ✅ Family mask cat([x·m,m]) — tracking pre-2013 masked not guessed
  • ✅ Procrustes root frame 1996-97 — RᵀR=I rotation chain, drift measured in degrees
  • ✅ Empirical-Bayes shrink % by attempts → shown as percentile not fake %
