Inside the network

Pick a player-season and walk through the MTNN — 120 stats in 17 families go through cat([x·m, m]) masking, 17 residual towers ×2 blocks (160→32 + LayerNorm + GELU + skip), concat with a 12-d season embedding (544+12=556 →128→48), L2-normalized to a 48-number fingerprint, then MLP heads fan out into archetype, skill, and next-year predictions. Click a node to trace its true path. Hit Play to watch the signal move.

Solo personal project — no connection to employer — built static, zero backend, free-tier only. Every number recomputable from stats.nba.com.
