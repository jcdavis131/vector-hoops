Sphere — L2 makes cosine honest

We divide by length. Every player is a point on a sphere. Similarity is just the dot product — angle, not raw height.

v̂ = v / ‖v‖₂, ‖v̂‖=1 · cos = v̂·ŵ = 1−‖v̂−ŵ‖²/2
Why: Length-normalized stops 30-min guys cheating via magnitude.
