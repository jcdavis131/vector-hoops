      How to play

      1. Every day we fuse two real player-seasons: one donates the counting stats, the other the shooting & impact profile. Name the real season that plays most like the blend.
      2. Guess any player-season; you get a % match, a court comparison, and directional coaching in sigmas.
      3. 92%+ match — or naming either component — wins. 6 guesses. New chimera at midnight UTC.
      4. Hints arrive on guesses 3 and 5 (Daily): a position-group chip, then an archetype-name chip. Free Play reveals both immediately — it's practice.
      5. Free Play (Chimera) and Free Play (The Deadline) are unlimited practice: they never touch your daily streak or stats.

      Anonymous play events help tune the game — a random id, no account, no ads.
