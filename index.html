How it works
  1. 01

    12,392 real player-seasons, 1996-97 through 2025-26, pulled from stats.nba.com per-100-possession splits.

  2. 02

    Every stat is sigmas vs that season's league — era-honest, so a 1999 season and a 2025 season compare fairly.

  3. 03

    You navigate the space: guess the fusion, call the swing, pick the teammate, order the arc — the game is the interface to the data.

Every number on this site is recomputable from source stats — an accuracy harness gates every deploy.
