        How to play

        1. Every day we fuse two real player-seasons: one donates the counting stats, the other the shooting & impact profile. Name the real season that plays most like the blend.
        2. Guess any player-season; you get a % match, a court comparison, and directional coaching in sigmas.
        3. 92%+ match — or naming either component — wins. 6 guesses. New chimera at midnight UTC.
        4. Hints arrive on guesses 3 and 5 (Daily): a position-group chip, then an archetype-name chip. Free Play reveals both immediately — it's practice.
        5. Free Play (all four modes) is unlimited practice: it never touches your daily streaks or stats.
        6. The Deadline: a real midseason mover's per-36/plus-minus swing is shown; call Thrived or Cratered. 5 rounds a day.
        7. Fader or Finisher: a real player-season's first-half per-36 rate is shown; call FINISHED STRONGER or FADED for the second half. 5 rounds a day.
        8. Career Arc: one player's charted seasons appear as unlabeled sigma-bar cards, no years shown. Tap them in the order you think they happened, oldest to newest, then submit — score is how many landed in the right slot.

        Anonymous play events help tune the game — a random id, no account, no ads.
