    Play-by-play

      What's real, what's staged

      Every event, clock, score, shooter and shot spot comes from the NBA's own play-by-play feed (source file). Shot spots match the feed's shot distances to the inch. The NBA doesn't publish where the other nine players stand, so they are staged from formation templates: offense spread around the arc, defenders between their man and the rim. Treat their spots as a sketch, not tracking data.
