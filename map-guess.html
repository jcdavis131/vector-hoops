How the hunt works

The mystery is one of 64 notable player-seasons, picked deterministically — the same star for everyone today. Distance is cosine distance in the 64-d MTNN v5 embedding (L2-normalized), the same space the twin engine uses. Twin Hunt hands you a player and asks for their era twin — the season from another decade that plays most alike, straight from the same twin data as Daily Court.

Every number recomputable from stats.nba.com · A dumbmodel project · Classic Daily Court · The atlas · Nearest seasons by cosine similarity in the 64-dimensional MTNN v5 embedding.
