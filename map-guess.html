How the hunt works

The mystery is one of 64 notable player-seasons, the same star for everyone today, picked by the calendar. Distance is cosine distance in the 64-d MTNN v5 embedding (L2-normalized), the same space the twin engine uses. Twin Hunt hands you a player and asks for their era twin — the season from another decade that plays most alike, straight from the same twin data as Daily Court.
