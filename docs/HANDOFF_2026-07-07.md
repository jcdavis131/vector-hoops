**Source:** `pipeline/data/mtnn_hp_sweep.json` (24 runs, 8 configs × 3 seeds, 40 ep).

| Finding | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Winner** | `concat-fusion-onecycle`: concat fusion, OneCycle linear, dim 48, lr 1.5e-3, nce_temp 0.08, drop 0.12 |
| **Best composite** | ~0.7445 (seed 42) |
| **Recall@10** | ~0.99–1.0 (saturated — not the bottleneck) |
| **Purity@20** | ~0.576 best vs **0.63 promotion floor** |
