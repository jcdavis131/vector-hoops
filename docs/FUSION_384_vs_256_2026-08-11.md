# The fusion-384 result does not survive a third seed

**2026-08-11.** Round 1's family search accepted nothing. The one candidate that
survived pairing was not a family — widening `--fusion-hidden` 256 -> 384 at dim
64 raised purity on both seeds it had been run on:

    e20, n=2    s7 +0.0187    s13 +0.0245

Against the 0.0042 purity seed sd this protocol measures, that reads as a 4.4
and 5.8 sd effect agreeing on both seeds, which is `same_sign`'s bar. **At n=2 a
sign test fires on a coin flip one time in four**, so it was never enough to
move a deployed model, which is why this was run.

## What a third seed does to it

    e10, paired    seed     dCQS     dPurity     dRecall
                   s5       +0.26    +0.0093     -0.012
                   s7       +0.83    +0.0116     +0.046
                   s13      +0.36    -0.0047     +0.010

    purity   mean +0.0054   sd 0.0088   positive 2/3   paired t +1.06
    cqs      mean +0.4833   sd 0.3044   positive 3/3   paired t +2.75
    recall   mean +0.0147   sd 0.0293   positive 2/3   paired t +0.87

**Purity is 2 of 3 and t = 1.06.** The effect that looked like four to six sd at
two seeds is not distinguishable from zero at three.

Seed 13 is the sharp part: **+0.0245 at 20 epochs, -0.0047 at 10.** Same seed,
same pair of configurations, opposite sign. Whatever the purity gain is, it is
not stable across the training budget.

## Why 10 epochs, and what that costs

A 20-epoch trial at this architecture takes longer than 108 seconds and the tool
budget here is 120, so an e20 trial cannot be completed in one call; a detached
parent gets about one child through and then stalls. Ten epochs fits.

That is a real limitation and it was checked rather than assumed: the first two
paired seeds at e10 gave purity +0.0093 and +0.0116 against e20's +0.0187 and
+0.0245 — **same sign, about half the size**. So e10 expresses the effect
attenuated rather than not at all, and a null here is informative rather than
merely underpowered. Seed 13 then flipped, which is the finding.

## Standing conclusion

Round 1 accepted nothing. Its one surviving candidate does not survive a third
seed. **Nothing from this search direction should be promoted**, and
`--write-artifacts` has never been passed, so nothing has been.

If it is worth another look, the thing to run is the same pair at e20 on four or
more seeds. That needs a way to run trials unattended, which this box currently
does not have: pipes versus files, CREATE_NO_WINDOW, console versus none, and a
delay between spawns have all been tried and none of them fixes the stall.
