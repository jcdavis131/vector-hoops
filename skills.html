Skills Lens

Every charted player-season graded 0–99 on twelve core skills — percentile within its own season, so a 90 in 1997 means exactly what a 90 in 2026 means. From 2015-16 on, six tracking skills (post play, transition, motor, and three gravity proxies) join the profile when synergy + hustle data exists. Badges fire at 90+, gold at 97+. Composites and caveats on the methods page.
