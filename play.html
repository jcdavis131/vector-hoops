                        How to play · era-honest MTNN v4

                        30-sec start · readable at all sizes

                        • Chimera: Name 3 real seasons — Stats + Style = Mashup closest real match in 12,966. Per-100 σ vs own season.
                        • All clues on screen Day 1 — orange right = above avg that year, blue left = below, center = avg. Sentence first.
                        • 8 games: Deadline thrived/cratered, Fader rose/fell per-36, Arc order career, Best Mate 4×5, What-If any eras coverage, Pivot adjacent role upside, Era Twin other decade 2 attempts warmth.
                        1. Chimera equation IS puzzle: Stats (pts/reb/ast/def) + Style (shooting & impact) = Mashup closest real season by 48-d L2. Stats/Style one guess each × multiplier, Mashup 6 tries 600→100 base × multipliers max 2400. Gold IDENTIFIED never red, silver ≥92% close enough also win.
                        2. Triangulation: Chimera diamond always shown in 3D map, donor squares hidden until stage resolves → spatial read. Court diagram zone presence orange offense brighter = further above avg, defense hatched = below.
                        3. Pool toggle: All-time 1996→2026 every season, Modern active only current season separate daily unranked, Free Play filters too. Shared links always All-time.
                        4. Deadline: real midseason mover per-36/plus-minus swing, call thrived/cratered 5 rounds. Method = Basketball-Reference game logs Δ.
                        5. Fader: first-half per-36 rate shown, call finished stronger or faded second half. Measured splits.
                        6. Career Arc: unlabeled sigma-bar cards no years, tap oldest→newest, scoring vs avg line chart reveal.
                        7. Best Teammate: teammate pair measured complementarity coverage map, pick true partner 4 options, reveal joint +/- percentile 5 rounds.
                        8. What-If Lab: any two eras partnership report complementarity dimensional coverage redundancy/weakness warnings combined court closest real duo — sandbox unlimited.
                        9. Pivot: current roster who has most measured upside in adjacent archetype role? Exact 2pts top3 1pt historical receipts not prediction 5 rounds.
                        10. Era Twin: anchor σ profile other decade double find them, typeahead other-decade only 2 attempts, warmth similarity % top5 shipped bound otherwise, side-by-side σ + age gap reveal 5 rounds.
