                        How to play

                        1. Every day we fuse two real player-seasons into one stat line — the equation IS the puzzle: Stats Player + Style Player = Mashup Player. All three cards show their full evidence (sigma bars, hint sentence, chips) from the start — nothing is hidden — but the ORDER is staged: one guess to name the Stats Player, then one guess to name the Style Player, then up to 6 guesses to name the Mashup. Each later card's input stays locked until the one before it resolves.
                        2. Stats/Style are single-shot: whatever you guess, the stage resolves immediately — right or wrong, the true donor is revealed with your alignment % and the multiplier it earned (exact player-season = ×2.0, ≥90% = ×1.5, ≥75% = ×1.25, ≥50% = ×1.1, below = ×1.0). The Mashup locks on the single closest real match in the whole pool (PERFECT MATCH, gold) or any guess ≥92% full-blend cosine ("close enough", silver) — solving on guess 1–6 is worth 600/500/400/300/200/100 base points. FINAL score = base × Stats multiplier × Style multiplier (max 2,400). Unsolved mashups still bank a consolation score off the two donor multipliers, so donor skill always counts.
                        3. Progressive triangulation: the Chimera diamond is always on the 3D map; each donor's anchor stays hidden until its stage resolves, then lights up — giving you a spatial read on whatever's still ahead.
                        4. Hints (Daily, Mashup stage only — both donors are already revealed by then): mashup guess 3 reveals the true match's position; guess 5 reveals its archetype.
                        5. A round is complete once the Mashup resolves (solved or out of guesses) — points post to the leaderboard either way. Free Play (Build-a-Chimera) is unchanged: you pick both donors yourself — the only hunt is finding the mashup's real match, unlimited attempts, none of it touches your daily streaks or stats.
                        6. The Deadline: a real midseason mover's per-36/plus-minus swing is shown; call Thrived or Cratered. 5 rounds a day.
                        7. Fader or Finisher: a real player-season's first-half per-36 rate is shown; call FINISHED STRONGER or FADED for the second half. 5 rounds a day.
                        8. Career Arc: one player's charted seasons appear as unlabeled sigma-bar cards, no years shown. Tap them in the order you think they happened, oldest to newest, then submit — score is how many landed in the right slot.
                        9. Chemistry: a real teammate pair (measured, same team-season) sets the question — "Who complemented {player} best on the {season} {team}?" Pick the true partner from 4 options; the reveal shows the measured complementarity, joint plus-minus, and a chemistry percentile, plus a one-line why. 5 rounds a day; unranked v1, doesn't touch the public leaderboard.
                        10. What-If Lab: pick any two player-seasons across any eras and get a full partnership report — complementarity, a dimensional coverage map, redundancy/shared-weakness warnings, a combined court diagram, and the closest real-world pair by measured complementarity. Pure sandbox: no daily puzzle, no streak.
                        11. The Pivot: a real current-roster team — "Who has the most measured upside in an ADJACENT role?" Pick a player; the reveal ranks every candidate by the real historical mean plus-minus swing for players who made that exact archetype-to-archetype pivot before, with a name-and-seasons example ("the receipts") and each candidate's own nearby archetypes. Exact pick = 2 pts, anyone in the top 3 = 1 pt, 5 rounds a day (0–10). Historical precedent with selection effects — not a prediction. Daily-set scores post to the public leaderboard.
                        12. Era Twin: every player has a geometric double in another decade — find them. An anchor player-season's sigma profile is shown; guess their era twin from a typeahead limited to other-decade players only, 2 attempts. First try = 2 pts, second try = 1 pt, miss = 0, 5 rounds a day (0–10). Warmth shows for near-misses: a guess in the shipped top 5 shows its real similarity %; anything else gets an honest "colder than" bound off the 5th-place similarity, since only the top 5 carry a shipped number. The reveal shows both players' sigma profiles side by side, the measured similarity, and the age gap in years. Daily-set scores post to the public leaderboard.
                        13. All-time / Modern toggle (top of the page): All-time draws from every charted season since 1996–97; Modern restricts puzzles to players active in the CURRENT NBA season (a mid-season rebuild picks this up automatically). Modern is a separate, parallel daily for Chimera, The Deadline, Fader or Finisher, Career Arc, Chemistry, and Era Twin — same date, its own puzzle, labeled "Modern — unranked" and never posted to the public leaderboard; your All-time daily is untouched and stays the ranked one. Toggling mid-day just swaps which daily you're looking at — neither is lost, and your Modern streaks/stats are tracked separately in the Stats modal. Free Play/pickers (Chimera, What-If Lab) filter to active players' seasons under Modern too. Era Twin anchors are Modern-only, but a twin can still be from any decade — that's the game. Career Arc requires ≥5 charted seasons even under Modern. The Deadline, Fader or Finisher, and Chemistry restrict to the last two charted seasons under Modern. The Pivot is unaffected (already scoped to current rosters). Shared challenge links always reproduce in All-time, regardless of your toggle.
