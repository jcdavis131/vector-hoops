One of these dots is a mystery

  • ✦The gold beacon is one real NBA season, 1996–2026, placed among all 12,966 by a neural net that only ever saw stats.
  • 📊Below the map is the machine's whole file on them — archetype pull, 12-skill DNA. No name, no team, no year.
  • 🎯Name the player in six. Every guess lands on the map and comes back with a warmth score — how close you are in the model's space.

How this works

The board. Every dot is one player-season, 1996-97 through 2025-26. Stats are taken per-100 possessions and z-scored within their own season, so eras compare honestly. A multi-tower neural net (MTNN v5) embeds each season into 48 dimensions; the map is a PCA projection of that space. Dots that sit together play alike — regardless of decade.

Warmth. Your guess is scored by cosine similarity to the mystery in the full 48-d space (not the 2-d picture). Warmth 97.3 means your guess's best-matching season is closer than 97.3% of all 12,966 seasons. For players with long careers, the game charitably uses whichever of their seasons sits nearest.

Intel unlocks. Miss 2: decade. Miss 3: position. Miss 4: exact season. Miss 5: last-name initial plus an honors note. All stated here — no hidden mechanics.

The daily. Everyone gets the same mystery, drawn from ~2,000 recognizable seasons (weighted by minutes, All-Star and All-NBA recognition). Practice rounds are unlimited and never touch your streak.

Honesty. Similarities run on int8-quantized embeddings; the worst-case drift vs full precision is measured and shipped in the data file (~0.012 cosine). Every grade and archetype traces to committed assets gated by tests. Dig deeper: the model · methods · the other 8 modes.
