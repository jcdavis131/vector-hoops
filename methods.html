1. Define — plain-English constructs

Construct validity asks: does number measure what we say? Front-office quality = ability to turn picks + dollars + foresight into wins + brand without cap hell April. Team strength = ability to hold past→modern in 48-d map. Scouting fit = twin realism: if map puts Giannis→LeBron closer than Giannis→Curry, scout nods.

FOR — Front Office Rating = 0.35·CapEfficiency +0.35·Flex +0.30·ForesightSurplus + timing + retained bonuses. Cap% = salary/cap 846 entries via cap_history 31 seasons $24,363,000→$154,647,000→$164,961,000 (2026-27 10% growth cap rule). Flex = draft weight .65·exp/2000+.35·1/√overall clip .06-3.5. Foresight = 3yr win delta vs market (appreciating cheap locks age-weighted).
W* = W+2.5·PO+0.12·realG — playoff wins 2.5× for brand (SAS 94.8 > OKC 85.8 example 2025-26). W/$B = W/$Val brand efficiency: Wins per $B valuation (GSW $9.14B top, 5.03 Wins/$B worst). Valα ±2 band flags over/under-valued.

Don't confuse: FOR 50 = median, 40-80 range, 70-80 top7% A+, not 0-100 vanity. W* 94.8 not 82 wins — includes playoff premium.
