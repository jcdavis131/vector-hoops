Stat Flow

How this season is built

Four stages, one story: the era it belongs to, the archetype it plays like, the three stats that define it, and the twin it mirrors across time.

Flow widths are proportional to |z-score| share of the three defining stats in the 14-dim era-honest style vector. The era twin is the nearest other-decade career in the 64-d embedding. Twin coverage spans careers with 4+ charted seasons — seasons outside the pool show an honest empty state, never a placeholder.

Methods and provenance: methods.html#insights · Every figure is computed from the served vectors, never invented.
