How to play
  • Think of any NBA player with a charted season since 1996 — 2,426 careers are in the tree.
  • Questions come from real career data: position, era, play style, Skills Lens tags, All-Star / All-NBA honours.
  • Every career resolves within 20 questions.
  • The map is the site’s 3D player map: one dot per season, placed by how that season was played. Tap a dot to see who it is.
  • Keyboard: Y yes, N no, U not sure.
