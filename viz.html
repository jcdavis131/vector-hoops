The radial twin graph, graduated from the Twin Networks launch page. Center node in gold, ring of nearest statistical twins, edge width proportional to cosine similarity in the 64-d embedding.

Primitive · flow

Stat Flow

The four-stage Sankey, graduated from the launch page: era → archetype → the three stats that define the season → the twin it mirrors across time. Link widths carry the |z-score| shares.

Primitive · bars

Era-Uniqueness Leaderboard

Distribution bars, graduated from the Insights card pattern. The strangest player-seasons in the dataset — ranked by distance from their own era's center.

Era-Uniqueness 0–100 = 1 − cosine(player, own-decade mean) in the L2-normalized MTNN v5 64-d embedding, minus the player's own season median (whole seasons drift together), min-maxed over all 12,966 player-seasons.

Primitive · drift

Era Drift Map

Every decade as a lane of uniqueness scores. Read the medians: which eras played alike, which ones scattered. The red dot is the player picked above.

All 12,966 player-seasons, binned per decade. Same Era-Uniqueness definition as above; medians are per-decade. Higher medians don't mean "better" — they mean the era's style was further from its own center.

Primitive · matchup · new

Twin Matchup

Two twin rings head to head. Twins of both light up gold; the strip between them carries the direct cosine similarity with a plain-words read.

Primitive · trajectory · new

Career Trajectory

A career as a path through the atlas plane — rookie to twilight. Scrub the seasons; the strip below says what changed, in plain words. Standalone: no atlas required.

Paths from the precomputed career trails (atlas x/y plane); deltas are the season-over-season stat shifts the trail builder charted. First entry is the rookie baseline.
