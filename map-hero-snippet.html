How to read this
  • Each dot is one player-season — 12,966 of them, positioned by our 64-dimensional player embedding, squashed into 3D so you can see it.
  • Closeness means similar playing style — dots near each other played the game in similar ways that season. That's all it means.
  • What it does NOT mean: the axes have no names — there is no "offense ↔ defense" axis. And the squashing distorts: t-SNE and UMAP keep local neighborhoods together but scramble distances between far-apart groups; PCA keeps the big-picture spread but can scramble neighbors. Pick the projection for the question you're asking.
  • Colors are k-means (k=8) clusters found by the algorithm on the full 64-d embeddings — not hand-drawn, and not positions or eras.
  • Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, click any dot to select that player-season.
