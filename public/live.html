AwayHome
How this is computed

Our model, stated plainly: p(home wins) = σ((lead + 3.0·f) / (13.5·√f)). Lead is the current home lead in points and f is the fraction of the game left, so the 3-point home-court edge fades to nothing by the buzzer. The shaded band is ±10 points of probability × √f: wide early, tight late. Experimental, and not fitted to historical data. The band is the point: it shows how much this crude model doesn’t know. The thin grey line is ESPN’s own model, shown for reference.
