WIN PROBABILITY

–
our experimental heuristic
AwayHome

Our model, stated plainly: p(home wins) = σ((lead + 3.0) / (13.5·√time-left)). Lead is the current home lead in points, time-left is the fraction of the game remaining, +3.0 is a fixed home-court edge. The shaded band is ±10pp·√time-left — wide early, tight late. Experimental heuristic, not fitted to historical data. The band is the point: it shows how much this crude model doesn’t know. Thin gray line = ESPN’s own model, shown for reference.
