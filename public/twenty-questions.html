How it works
  • Think of any NBA player with a charted season since 1996 — 2,426 careers are in the tree.
  • Questions come from real career data: position, era, play style, Skills Lens tags, All-Star / All-NBA honours.
  • The tree is built offline for maximum information gain; every career resolves within 20 questions (worst case observed: 18).
  • The map plots all 12,966 player-seasons as a 2-D projection of the 14 era-normalised per-100 features — the site’s atlas view, not the 64-d MTNN embedding itself.
  • Keyboard: Y yes, N no, U not sure.

Provenance: tree built 2026-10-20 from assets/vectors.json, assets/archetype_assignments.json, assets/honors.json — greedy max-information-gain, worst case 18 questions. Map: 12,966 player-seasons, PCA of the 14 era-normalised per-100 features.
