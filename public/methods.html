construct validity plain-English

What we claim and how we measure it

Construct validity asks: does our number really measure what we say? A construct is the plain-English idea like front-office quality or team strength. Operationalization is the specific math we use. We test convergence (our measure agrees with others it should), discriminant (doesn't agree with unrelated), predictive (forecasts future).

  • FOR — Front Office Rating = 0.35·CapEfficiency + 0.35·FlexScore + 0.30·ForesightSurplus. Cap% = salary/cap 846 entries, flex = draft pick surplus + trade flex, foresight = 3yr win delta vs market.
  • W* — Championship-adjusted wins = W + 2.5·PO_Wins + 0.12·realGames. Playoffs count 2.5× for brand value. SAS 94.8 W* 2023-24 example.
  • OU baseline r=0.741 : Vegas over/under 944 seasons correlates 0.741 to true wins. We must beat this baseline. Mitigation uses effective cap (cap_history 31 seasons) to adjust payroll context.
  • W/$B = W/$Val brand efficiency: Wins per $B valuation. Valα ±2 band flags over/under-valued teams.
