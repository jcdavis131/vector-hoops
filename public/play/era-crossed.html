How it works
  • Each anchor is a real past career (4+ charted seasons) with a signature season.
  • The correct answer is the nearest other-decade career by 64-d MTNN embedding cosine — the same twin the site’s Twin Networks use.
  • The three wrong options aren’t random: they’re the next-closest shipped candidates, so every round is genuinely hard.
  • Similarity shown after you pick is embedding cosine × 100 — a closeness score, not a percentage of anything.
  • Streak: consecutive games at 7+/10. Saved on this device only.

Provenance: quiz built 2026-10-20 from assets/eratwins.json — twin = nearest other-decade career by 64-d MTNN embedding cosine; distractors = 3 of the other 4 shipped top-5 candidates. 1,313 rounds pre-dealt into 131 games at build time — only one game's rounds (~4KB) download per visit.
