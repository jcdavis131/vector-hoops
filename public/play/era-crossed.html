How to play
  • Each anchor is a real past career (4+ charted seasons) with a signature season.
  • The correct answer is the nearest career from another decade — the same twin the site’s Twin Networks use. Twins match full stat-plus-stardom profiles — a statistical neighbor is not necessarily the same position or role.
  • The three wrong options aren’t random: they’re the next-closest shipped candidates, so every round is genuinely hard.
  • The similarity shown after you pick is a closeness score out of 100, not a percentage of anything.
  • Streak: consecutive games at 7+/10. Saved on this device only.
