How it's scored: per-100 playoff scoring plus shooting efficiency, then +2 per round won and +4 for the title — winning matters, but it never swamps the per-possession signal. Duos and trios are the highest-scoring units whose members each played real rotation roles (40+ regular-season games, 12+ playoff games — every unit here won a series). Fives and starters aren't ranked: the source minutes field can't identify them, so we don't fake them. Every figure recomputes from stats.nba.com splits, 1996-97 → 2025-26.
