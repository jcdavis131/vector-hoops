Bird rights & soft cap — plain English

Soft cap: you can go over to re-sign your own free agents using Bird Rights. 3 years with same team = full Bird, you can exceed cap any amount up to max. Early Bird (2 years) = 175% of prior salary. Non-Bird (1 year) = 120%. This is why older flat deals signed when cap $90-110M now at $154M are steals — cap grew faster than salary.
